Step 1: Click the button below to open the chess app in a new tab
Step 2: Follow the step-by-step instructions below to play chess

📋 Step-by-Step Chess Instructions

1. Start the Game

  • Open the chess app
  • Choose "🤖 Play vs Computer" or "👥 Play vs Friend"
  • If playing vs Computer, select difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard
  • The game board will appear with chess pieces

2. Understand the Board

  • White pieces are at the bottom, Black pieces at the top
  • You play as White (bottom) when vs Computer
  • Each piece type: King (♔♚), Queen (♕♛), Rook (♖♜), Bishop (♗♝), Knight (♘♞), Pawn (♙♟)
  • The board shows "Your turn" or "Computer's turn" status

3. Make a Move

  • Click and drag any White piece to move it
  • Valid destination squares will be highlighted
  • Release the piece on a valid square
  • Illegal moves will snap back to original position
  • The move will be recorded and shown in status

4. Computer Response

  • After your move, Computer will automatically move
  • Computer's move appears after ~1 second
  • Game status shows "Computer played: [move]"
  • Then it's your turn again

5. Game Controls

  • ↶ Undo - Undo the last move (yours + computer's)
  • 🔄 Reset - Start a new game with same settings
  • 🏠 Menu - Return to main menu
  • New Game - Start fresh from header

6. Winning the Game

  • Checkmate: Trap the opponent's King - you win!
  • Stalemate: No legal moves - draw!
  • Check: King is under attack (warning)
  • Game ends automatically when checkmate/draw occurs

🧪 Quick Test Moves

Try These First Moves:

  1. Move White pawn from e2 to e4 (drag forward 2 squares)
  2. Computer will respond (usually with e5 or d6)
  3. Move White Knight from g1 to f3 (L-shape move)
  4. Continue playing - the game should flow smoothly!

🐛 Troubleshooting

If Pieces Don't Move:

  • Make sure you're dragging White pieces (bottom)
  • Check if it's your turn (status shows)
  • Try a different piece - some pieces have limited moves
  • Refresh the page and start a new game
